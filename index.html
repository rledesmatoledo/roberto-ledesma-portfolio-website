About Me

Hi, I'm Roberto Ledesma, a first-generation Computer Science student at San Francisco State University, graduating in May 2027. I'm mainly interested in software engineering and web development, and possibly UI/UX design too.

My interest in programming started in high school, when a website for a class project got me hooked. Since then I've built a Discord bot just for fun, a Kanban task manager and a Twitter clone for my classes, and an AI assistant that answers financial questions using real SEC filings through my Management Leadership for Tomorrow (MLT) program.

I'm an MLT Career Prep Fellow, and I'm looking for software engineering opportunities where I can keep building.

Outside of tech, I love taking photos and shooting cinematic videos with my digital camera, and teaching myself new things. I'm always happy to connect with people in tech to swap ideas and learn from different perspectives.
